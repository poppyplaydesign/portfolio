Kitchen Adventures is a hands-on culinary subscription kit for Little Passports, designed for kids aged 5–12. Each kit takes children on a journey to a different country through its cuisine — with age-appropriate recipes, kid-safe tools, specialty ingredients, and illustrated activity cards that bring the culture and food to life.

As Senior Product Designer, I led the end-to-end product development process: from concept and curriculum design to packaging, sourcing, and manufacturing coordination. The project required balancing safety standards for young children, supply chain constraints, and an engaging narrative that made cooking feel like a global adventure. The kits received strong customer response and became one of the brand's top-performing subscription lines.
