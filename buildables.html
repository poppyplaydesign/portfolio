Buildables is a STEM construction toy line for Skillmatics, developed for children aged 4–10. The range features interlocking components that guide children through step-by-step builds — vehicles, structures, and machines — while simultaneously teaching foundational engineering concepts through play.

I managed product development across several SKUs in the Buildables line, from initial briefs and component design to safety testing, packaging, and go-to-market planning. The key design challenge was creating a system that felt satisfying and achievable for younger children while remaining open-ended and exploratory enough to grow with the child. Buildables launched to strong retail reception and became a cornerstone of Skillmatics' STEM product portfolio.
