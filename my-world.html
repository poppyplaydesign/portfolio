My World is a DIY pretend play kit for Skillmatics, designed to nurture imaginative and creative play in young children aged 3–7. The kit gives kids everything they need to build and decorate their own miniature world — houses, characters, vehicles, and settings — encouraging open-ended storytelling and social play.

As Product Manager, I oversaw the full product lifecycle from initial concept through to launch, collaborating closely with designers, illustrators, and manufacturers. A key challenge was designing components that were safe, durable, and engaging for a wide age range, while keeping the activity accessible and screen-free. My World became one of Skillmatics' most appreciated products in its launch year.
