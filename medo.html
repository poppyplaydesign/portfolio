Medo is a concept toy that bridges physical and digital play using augmented reality. The toy consists of a series of layered, semi-transparent physical components — when viewed through a companion app, each layer reveals an animated character world hidden within. As children add or remove layers, the AR scene changes, rewarding curiosity and exploration with surprising visual moments.

This project was a speculative design exploration into how physical toys could integrate AR in a way that felt magical rather than gimmicky. The design philosophy prioritised the physical toy as the primary experience, with AR serving as a reward layer rather than a dependency — ensuring the toy remained meaningful without a screen. Medo demonstrated strong potential for phygital product innovation in the preschool and early childhood categories.
