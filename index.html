About Me

Designing for delight,
one play experience at a time.

I'm a toy & game designer specialising in construction toys, STEM kits, and tabletop games — designed for ages 3 and up. I believe tactile interactivity and physical feedback are some of the most powerful tools for learning and joy.

My career spans Product Management and Senior Design roles at globally loved brands — from Skillmatics in India to Little Passports and Thames & Kosmos in the US. I bring both creative vision and product rigour to everything I make.

Poppy Play Design is my freelance studio, where I work with brands to conceive, develop, and launch play experiences that kids and families love.
